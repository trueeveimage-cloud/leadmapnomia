## KPIs

- Time to first paid account under 30 days.
- Trial-to-paid conversion 15-30%.
- False-positive rate under 10% of sent alerts.
- High-severity approval time under 4 business hours.
- Monthly churn under 5% after month two.
- Source scan success above 98%.
- Median source-to-alert latency under 60 minutes.
